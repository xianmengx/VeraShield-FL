# 20% Byzantine Multi-Seed Configuration Audit

No training was started during this audit.

## Locked main configuration

- Dataset: CIFAR-10
- Clients: 20, full participation
- Rounds: 60
- Byzantine ratio: 0.2 (4 fixed malicious clients per run)
- Attack start: round 4; rounds 1--3 are benign calibration rounds
- Attacks: `sign_flip` (strength 5), `random_gradient` (strength 10), and
  `scaling` (strength 10)
- Seeds: 7, 21, and 42
- Dropout: 0
- IID: `partitioner=iid`, `alpha=1.0`
- Non-IID: `partitioner=dirichlet`, `alpha=0.5`
- Krum: fixed defense budget `f=2`
- Trimmed Mean: `trim_ratio=0.2`; with 20 available clients this removes
  two low and two high values per coordinate
- Malicious-client selection seed: 42, held fixed across methods and training
  seeds so paired comparisons use the same attacker identities
- Server tampering: disabled in this client-poisoning matrix

## Provenance correction

Historical manifests generated by `scripts/gen_scenario.py` recorded
`training_config.partitioner=iid` even for Dirichlet runs and omitted `alpha`.
The generator now accepts `--partitioner` and `--alpha` and writes both at the
top level and under `training_config`. Existing result files are preserved and
are not rewritten.

## Existing-result reuse

- IID seed 7: the three attacks and five methods exist with the locked attack
  settings. Use the historical Krum `f=2` and Trimmed Mean `trim_ratio=0.2`
  directories, not the later oracle-matched `f=4` / `trim_ratio=0.4` runs.
- Non-IID seed 7 sign-flip: VeraShield-FL, FedAvg, Trimmed Mean, and FedDMC are
  reusable for performance. Their copied manifest has incorrect partitioner
  provenance, so the runtime `config.json` is the authoritative partition
  record and the limitation must be disclosed.
- Non-IID seed 7 sign-flip Krum is not reusable in the fixed-budget matrix
  because it used `f=4`; rerun this one cell with `f=2`.
- Non-IID seed 7 random-gradient and scaling have not passed the required
  same-budget prevalidation and must be run before multi-seed expansion.

## Expansion gate

Before seeds 21 and 42 are launched for a non-IID attack, seed 7 must show:

- VeraShield-FL recall at least 0.90;
- benign FPR at most 0.05;
- no empty accepted set;
- no Crypto-VL verification failure;
- no secret-recovery failure.
