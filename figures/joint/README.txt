CIFAR-10 / MNIST: Test accuracy over communication rounds under the joint-threat scenario. Each curve is the arithmetic mean of three runs (seeds 7, 21, and 42); round 0 is initialization. No smoothing or uncertainty shading is applied. VeraShield-FL uses the latest precommit-verification runs; the four baselines use the existing joint-scenario runs. Server tampering is scheduled at rounds 20, 40, and 59. For VeraShield-FL, rejected candidates are not installed, and accuracy is evaluated on the retained committed model. Final-round per-seed accuracy and mean/sample SD are reported in final_accuracy_summary.csv.

These are logical-prototype utility results; the curves do not establish physical two-server privacy isolation. Baseline instability and differences in actual tamper execution must be discussed separately in the experiment text.
